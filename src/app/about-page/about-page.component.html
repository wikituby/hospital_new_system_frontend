Veneranda Hospital

Veneranda Hospital offers services ranging from maternity and paediatrics to ultrasound and other scan types, general doctor consultation, pharmacy, full laboratory services, and dental care. A full-time doctor and a full-time dentist are available for appointments. We are growing — theatre and surgical services, blood bank, and ambulance support are on the way.
