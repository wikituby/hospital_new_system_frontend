How to create expense accounts

  1. Open the Categories tab and create at least one expense category (e.g. Utilities, Transport).
  2. Return to Accounts and click New Account.
  3. Choose a category, enter the account name (e.g. Electricity, Fuel), and optional description.
  4. Click Save Account — the account appears in this list and can be used when recording expenses.

While recording an expense, you can also search accounts or create a new one from the expense account dropdown (first row: search + New account).
